## 1.1.1
- The meter and its DAMAGE tab hide while the pause menu is open.
- Player colours now come through for other players in multiplayer.

## 1.1.0
- Show or hide the meter with the mouse: an **X** button on the meter hides it, leaving a small **DAMAGE** tab you click to bring it back. A **Spells** button turns the per-spell lines on or off. F3 and F4 still work.

## 1.0.0
- First release: a live meter with per-player DPS, total damage and share, plus a per-spell breakdown. The host's numbers are shared with all players.
